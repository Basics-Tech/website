What we do
AI and technology should work for everyone
We focus on the areas where technology can have the greatest social impact — and where the gap between those who have access and those who don't is growing fastest.
🔍

AI Transparency & Accountability

Open source tools to audit AI systems that make decisions about people's lives. Making the EU AI Act actionable for communities, not just corporations.

♿

Digital Accessibility

Bridging the EU AI Act and the European Accessibility Act. Ensuring AI systems work for the 87 million people with disabilities in Europe.

📱

Digital Literacy

AI-powered tools to help older adults, migrants, and vulnerable communities navigate the digital world. Meet people where they are — WhatsApp, voice, their language.

🏥

Community Health

Training programs and digital tools for community health workers, caregivers, and local governments. Health literacy through technology.

🏛

Democratic AI Governance

Platforms for citizen participation in AI policy. Monitoring tools for public AI systems. Evidence for advocacy and civil society.

💼

Labour Impact of AI

Tools to help workers and unions understand automation risk, identify transferable skills, and access retraining. Especially for workers over 45.

Our mission

Why this matters now

AI is advancing faster than society can adapt. The benefits are concentrating in fewer hands. The communities most affected by automation have the least access to the tools shaping their future. We're here to change that.

  • Less than 0.1% of AI investment goes to social impact
  • 87 million Europeans with disabilities are excluded from AI design
  • 57% of working hours are automatable with current technology
  • The EU AI Act deadline for high-risk systems is August 2026
  • Open source is infrastructure, not charity
